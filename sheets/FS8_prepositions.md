## Part 1 · The core problem for Spanish speakers / El problema central para hispanohablantes

**EN:** Spanish has one word — **en** — where English has three: **in, on, at**. *En la mesa, en Madrid, en casa* → *ON the table, IN Madrid, AT home.* There is no translation shortcut; instead, English uses a **mental picture**, and once you see the picture, most cases become predictable:

* **in** = inside a **container** (a box, a room, a city, a year — anything with an "inside")
* **on** = touching a **surface** (a table, a wall, a street — a flat contact)
* **at** = at a **point** on the map (a specific spot or address — no inside, no surface, just a location dot)

**ES:** El español tiene una palabra — **en** — donde el inglés tiene tres: **in, on, at**. No hay atajo de traducción; el inglés usa una **imagen mental**, y cuando ves la imagen, la mayoría de los casos se vuelven predecibles:

* **in** = dentro de un **contenedor** (una caja, un cuarto, una ciudad, un año — algo con "adentro")
* **on** = tocando una **superficie** (una mesa, una pared, una calle — contacto plano)
* **at** = en un **punto** del mapa (un lugar específico — sin adentro ni superficie, solo un punto)

## Part 2 · Place / Lugar

| Preposition | Picture / Imagen | Examples | Español |
|---|---|---|---|
| **in** | container / contenedor | in the room, in the car, in my bag, in Madrid, in Colombia, in the kitchen | en el cuarto, en el coche, en Madrid |
| **on** | surface / superficie | on the table, on the wall, on the floor, on the second floor, on Main Street | en la mesa, en la pared, en el segundo piso |
| **at** | point / punto | at home, at school, at work, at the door, at the bus stop, at the airport | en casa, en la escuela, en la puerta |

**EN:** Special cases to memorize (they follow history, not the picture): **on** the bus / train / plane (public transport = on) but **in** the car / taxi. **At** home, **at** work, **at** school — fixed expressions with no article.
**ES:** Casos especiales para memorizar: **on** the bus / train / plane (transporte público = on) pero **in** the car / taxi. **At** home, **at** work, **at** school — expresiones fijas sin artículo.

## Part 3 · Time / Tiempo

**EN:** Time uses the same three words with a size logic: **in** for big containers of time, **on** for days (one calendar square), **at** for exact points on the clock.

**ES:** El tiempo usa las mismas tres palabras con una lógica de tamaño: **in** para contenedores grandes de tiempo, **on** para días (una casilla del calendario), **at** para puntos exactos del reloj.

| Preposition | Size / Tamaño | Examples | Español |
|---|---|---|---|
| **in** | months, years, seasons, parts of day / meses, años, estaciones, partes del día | in July, in 2026, in summer, in the morning/afternoon/evening | en julio, en 2026, en verano, por la mañana |
| **on** | days & dates / días y fechas | on Monday, on July 10th, on my birthday, on the weekend (AmE) | el lunes, el 10 de julio |
| **at** | clock times & fixed points / horas y puntos fijos | at 5 o'clock, at 7:30, at noon, at midnight, **at night** | a las 5, al mediodía, por la noche |

⚠️ *In the morning* but *at night* — an irregular pair everyone must simply memorize. ⚠️ No preposition before *today, tomorrow, yesterday, next week, last year, every day*: ✗ ~~in the next week~~ → ✅ *next week*.

## Part 4 · Preposition + verb = gerund, always / Preposición + verbo = gerund, siempre

**EN:** Reminder from the gerunds sheet, because it never stops being tested: if a verb comes right after **any** preposition, it wears -ing. *Interested **in learning**, tired **of waiting**, **before leaving**, thank you **for helping**.* Never the infinitive: ✗ ~~before to leave~~.
**ES:** Recordatorio de la hoja de gerunds, porque nunca deja de evaluarse: si un verbo viene justo después de **cualquier** preposición, lleva -ing. Nunca el infinitivo: ✗ ~~before to leave~~ → ✅ *before leaving*.

## Part 5 · Common mistakes / Errores comunes

| ❌ | ✅ | Why / Por qué |
|---|---|---|
| I'll see you in Monday. | ...**on** Monday. | days → on |
| She was born on 1998. | ...**in** 1998. | years → in |
| The book is in the table. | ...**on** the table. | surface → on |
| He is in home. | He is **at** home. | fixed point expression |
| We arrived at Madrid. | We arrived **in** Madrid. | cities are containers (arrive in a city, at a small place) |
| I study in the night. | ...**at** night. | fixed pair: in the morning / at night |
| I'm thinking in you. | I'm thinking **about/of** you. | "pensar en" ≠ think in |

*Remember / Recuerda:* don't translate *en* — draw the picture: container → **in**, surface → **on**, point → **at**. Time follows size: big (months/years) → in, days → on, clock → at. And after any preposition, a verb wears -ing. / No traduzcas *en* — dibuja la imagen: contenedor → **in**, superficie → **on**, punto → **at**. El tiempo sigue el tamaño: grande (meses/años) → in, días → on, reloj → at. Y después de cualquier preposición, el verbo lleva -ing.
