## Part 1 · Two futures, two flavors / Dos futuros, dos sabores

**EN:** English has two main ways to talk about the future, and the difference is **when you decided**:

* **be going to + verb** → a **plan** that existed *before* speaking, or a prediction based on evidence you can see. *I'**m going to visit** my parents next month* (already planned). *Look at those clouds — it'**s going to rain*** (evidence).
* **will + verb** → a decision made **at this moment**, a promise, an offer, or an opinion-prediction. *The phone is ringing — I'**ll answer** it!* (deciding now). *I **will help** you, I promise.* *I think it **will be** a good year.*

Spanish parallel — and it's a friendly one: *be going to* maps word-for-word to **"ir a + infinitivo"** (*voy a comprar* = *I'm going to buy*), and *will* roughly to the future simple (*compraré*). Your instinct from Spanish will usually pick correctly.

**ES:** El inglés tiene dos formas principales de hablar del futuro, y la diferencia es **cuándo decidiste**:

* **be going to + verbo** → un **plan** que existía *antes* de hablar, o una predicción con evidencia visible. *Voy a visitar a mis padres* (ya planeado). *Mira esas nubes — va a llover* (evidencia).
* **will + verbo** → una decisión tomada **en este momento**, una promesa, un ofrecimiento, o una predicción-opinión. *¡Suena el teléfono — yo contesto!* (decides ahora).

Paralelo con el español — y es amistoso: *be going to* se traduce palabra por palabra como **"ir a + infinitivo"** (*voy a comprar* = *I'm going to buy*), y *will* más o menos como el futuro simple (*compraré*). Tu instinto del español normalmente elegirá bien.

## Part 2 · The forms / Las formas

**be going to** — a three-part engine: *be* (helper, changes with subject) + *going to* + BASE verb.

| SUBJECT | be | going to | BASE verb | Español |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | am ('m) | going to | travel in July. | Voy a viajar en julio. |
| She | is ('s) | going to | study tonight. | Va a estudiar esta noche. |
| They | are ('re) | going to | move next year. | Van a mudarse el próximo año. |

Because the helper is *be*, negatives and questions use the **FS-1 machine**: *She **isn't going to** study. / **Are you going to** travel?* Never *do*: ✗ *Do you going to...?*

**will** — one word for **every** subject + BASE verb. (*will* is your first modal — full family in FS-9.)

| | Example | Español |
|---|---|---|
| affirmative | I/She/They **will call** you. (I'll, she'll, they'll) | Te llamaré / llamará / llamarán. |
| negative | I **won't** forget. (will not → won't) | No olvidaré. |
| question | **Will you help** me? — Yes, I **will**. / No, I **won't**. | ¿Me ayudarás? |

⚠️ Two laws of *will*: (1) never *to* after it — ✗ *I will to go* → ✅ *I will go*; (2) never -s — ✗ *She will goes* → ✅ *She will go*.

### Mini-practice 1: will or going to? 1. I already have tickets. I ___ (see) a concert on Friday. 2. "The bag is heavy." — "I ___ (carry) it for you!" 3. Look at the sky! It ___ (rain).

## Part 3 · The three "going to" patterns, finally united / Los tres patrones de "going to", por fin unidos

**EN:** You have now met "going" in three different machines. Here they are side by side — this resolves the original *"going to shopping"* confusion completely:

| Pattern | Example | What "going" is doing |
|---|---|---|
| go **to** + place | I go **to the mall**. | movement to a place / movimiento a un lugar |
| go + activity(**-ing**), no "to" | I go **shopping** on Saturdays. | fixed activity pattern (FS-X) |
| be **going to** + BASE verb | I'm **going to shop** tomorrow. | FUTURE plan / plan futuro |

And yes, they combine: *I'm **going to go shopping** tomorrow.* ✅ (future + activity) — perfectly correct and very common.

**ES:** Ya conociste "going" en tres máquinas diferentes. Aquí están lado a lado — esto resuelve por completo la confusión original de *"going to shopping"*. Y sí, se combinan: *I'm **going to go shopping** tomorrow.* ✅ (futuro + actividad) — perfectamente correcto y muy común.

## Part 4 · Bonus: the continuous as future / Bono: el continuo como futuro

**EN:** For **fixed arrangements** (with a time/place already set, often with other people), English also uses the present continuous: *I'**m meeting** Ana at 6.* / *We'**re flying** to Miami on Friday.* Spanish does the same thing (*Mañana como con mi madre*), so this will feel natural. Don't worry about choosing between this and *going to* — at this level they overlap safely.

**ES:** Para **compromisos fijos** (con hora/lugar ya establecidos, a menudo con otras personas), el inglés también usa el presente continuo: *I'**m meeting** Ana at 6.* El español hace lo mismo (*Mañana como con mi madre*), así que esto te resultará natural. No te preocupes por elegir entre esto y *going to* — a este nivel se superponen sin peligro.

## Part 5 · Common mistakes / Errores comunes

| ❌ | ✅ | Why / Por qué |
|---|---|---|
| I will to call you. | I will **call** you. | never *to* after will |
| She will goes home. | She will **go** home. | base verb after will |
| I going to travel. | I **am** going to travel. | the *be* helper is required |
| Do you going to come? | **Are** you going to come? | *be* machine, not *do* machine |
| I no will forget. | I **won't** forget. | negation lives inside *won't* |
| I'm going to shopping. | I'm going **shopping**. (activity) / I'm going **to shop**. (future) | don't fuse the patterns / no fusiones los patrones |
