# Foundation Sheet 1 · The Verb *Be*
# Hoja de Fundamentos 1 · El Verbo *Be*

**Prerequisite / Prerrequisito:** FS-0 (the seats / los asientos).

---

## Part 1 · Why *be* is special / Por qué *be* es especial

**EN:** Most verbs describe an **action** (eat, run, study). *Be* is different: it describes a **state** — what something *is*, not what it *does*. It connects the subject to a description: *The coffee is hot.* Because it is a state, Pattern B from FS-0 uses it: SUBJECT + be + DESCRIPTION. *Be* is also the most irregular verb in English: it changes shape depending on the subject. You must know its shapes by heart, because half of beginner English runs on this one verb.

**ES:** La mayoría de los verbos describen una **acción** (comer, correr, estudiar). *Be* es diferente: describe un **estado** — lo que algo *es*, no lo que *hace*. Conecta al sujeto con una descripción: *The coffee is hot.* Por eso usa el Patrón B de FS-0: SUJETO + be + DESCRIPCIÓN. Además, *be* es el verbo más irregular del inglés: cambia de forma según el sujeto. Tienes que saber sus formas de memoria, porque la mitad del inglés básico funciona con este único verbo.

⚠️ **EN:** Spanish has TWO verbs here — *ser* and *estar*. English has only ONE: *be* does both jobs. *Soy ingeniera* → *I **am** an engineer.* *Estoy cansada* → *I **am** tired.* Good news: you never have to choose.
**ES:** El español tiene DOS verbos aquí — *ser* y *estar*. El inglés tiene solo UNO: *be* hace los dos trabajos. Buena noticia: nunca tienes que elegir.

## Part 2 · The three shapes / Las tres formas

| Subject / Sujeto | Form / Forma | Contraction / Contracción | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | **am** | I'm | I am tired. → I'm tired. |
| he / she / it (+ any single thing) | **is** | he's, she's, it's, Maria's | She is a doctor. → She's a doctor. |
| you / we / they (+ any plural) | **are** | you're, we're, they're | They are happy. → They're happy. |

**EN:** Contractions (I'm, she's...) are normal spoken and written English — not lazy English. Use them.
**ES:** Las contracciones (I'm, she's...) son inglés normal, hablado y escrito — no es inglés perezoso. Úsalas.

### Mini-practice 1 / Mini-práctica 1 — am, is or are?

1. My sister ___ a nurse.  2. I ___ from Colombia.  3. The keys ___ on the table.  4. It ___ cold today.

## Part 3 · Negatives: add *not* / Negaciones: añade *not*

**EN:** To make *be* negative, put **not** right after it. That's all — no helper word needed.
**ES:** Para negar *be*, pon **not** justo después. Eso es todo — no se necesita palabra auxiliar.

| Full form | Contraction / Contracción | Español |
|---|---|---|
| I am not tired. | I'**m not** tired. | No estoy cansada. |
| She is not a teacher. | She **isn't** a teacher. / She'**s not**... | No es profesora. |
| They are not here. | They **aren't** here. / They'**re not**... | No están aquí. |

⚠️ There is no "amn't": *I am not* → only *I'm not*.

## Part 4 · Questions: flip the seats / Preguntas: invierte los asientos

**EN:** This is the first question machine of English, and it is beautifully simple: to ask a question with *be*, **swap the subject and the verb**. Nothing is added, nothing changes shape.

| Statement / Afirmación | Question / Pregunta |
|---|---|
| **She is** a doctor. | **Is she** a doctor? |
| **They are** at home. | **Are they** at home? |
| **I am** late. | **Am I** late? |

Short answers repeat the *be*, nothing more: *Is she a doctor?* → *Yes, she **is**. / No, she **isn't**.* (Never ✗ *Yes, she's* — the contraction can't end the sentence.)

**ES:** Esta es la primera máquina de preguntas del inglés, y es muy simple: para preguntar con *be*, **intercambia el sujeto y el verbo**. No se añade nada, nada cambia de forma. Las respuestas cortas repiten el *be* y nada más: *Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.* (Nunca ✗ *Yes, she's* — la contracción no puede terminar la oración.)

⚠️ **EN:** In Spanish a question can be just intonation: *¿Ella es doctora?* In written English homework, you must flip: *Is she a doctor?*
**ES:** En español una pregunta puede ser solo entonación: *¿Ella es doctora?* En la tarea escrita de inglés, debes invertir: *Is she a doctor?*

## Part 5 · *There is / There are* = "hay" / *There is / There are* = "hay"

**EN:** To say that something **exists** somewhere, English uses *there is* (one thing) or *there are* (more than one). Spanish uses one word for both — *hay* — so the choice singular/plural is the new skill.

**ES:** Para decir que algo **existe** en un lugar, el inglés usa *there is* (una cosa) o *there are* (más de una). El español usa una sola palabra para ambos — *hay* — así que elegir singular/plural es la habilidad nueva.

| | Example | Español |
|---|---|---|
| one thing / una cosa | **There is** a park near my house. | Hay un parque cerca de mi casa. |
| plural / plural | **There are** two banks on this street. | Hay dos bancos en esta calle. |
| negative | There **isn't** a pool. / There **aren't** any buses. | No hay piscina. / No hay autobuses. |
| question | **Is there** a pharmacy? **Are there** shops? | ¿Hay una farmacia? ¿Hay tiendas? |

⚠️ Don't translate *hay* as ✗ *have*: ✗ *Have a park near my house* → ✅ *There is a park near my house.*

## Part 6 · The *tener* trap / La trampa de *tener*

**EN:** Spanish uses *tener* (have) for many states where English uses ***be***. Memorize this table — these are among the most common Spanish-speaker errors in existence:

**ES:** El español usa *tener* para muchos estados donde el inglés usa ***be***. Memoriza esta tabla — están entre los errores más comunes de los hispanohablantes:

| Español | ❌ Wrong | ✅ Correct |
|---|---|---|
| Tengo 25 años. | ~~I have 25 years.~~ | I **am** 25 (years old). |
| Tengo hambre. | ~~I have hunger.~~ | I **am** hungry. |
| Tengo sed. | ~~I have thirst.~~ | I **am** thirsty. |
| Tengo frío / calor. | ~~I have cold/hot.~~ | I **am** cold / hot. |
| Tengo miedo. | ~~I have fear.~~ | I **am** afraid / scared. |
| Tienes razón. | ~~You have reason.~~ | You **are** right. |

## Part 7 · Common mistakes / Errores comunes

| ❌ | ✅ | Why / Por qué |
|---|---|---|
| Is cold today. | **It** is cold today. | subject seat never empty (FS-0) / el sujeto nunca se omite |
| She no is happy. | She **is not (isn't)** happy. | negation = be + not, "no" doesn't exist here |
| She's doctor. | She's **a** doctor. | professions need *a/an* (FS-6) / las profesiones llevan *a/an* |
| They is my friends. | They **are** my friends. | plural subject → are |
| Have three rooms in the house. | **There are** three rooms in the house. | hay = there is/are, not have |
| You are tired? | **Are you** tired? | written questions flip / las preguntas escritas invierten |

## Part 8 · Practice / Práctica
