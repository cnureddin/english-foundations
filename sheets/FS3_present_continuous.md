# Foundation Sheet 3 · Present Continuous vs. Simple Present
# Hoja de Fundamentos 3 · Presente Continuo vs. Presente Simple

**Prerequisites / Prerrequisitos:** FS-0, FS-1 (*be*), FS-2 (simple present). Cross-links / Se conecta con: FS-X (gerunds — "the other -ing").

---

## Part 1 · Two presents, two meanings / Dos presentes, dos significados

**EN:** English has two "present" tenses, and they answer different questions:

* **Simple present** (FS-2) answers: *"What is generally true?"* — habits, routines, facts. *I work in a hospital* (that's my job).
* **Present continuous** answers: *"What is happening right now / temporarily?"* — *I am working* (at this moment / these days).

Spanish often uses the simple present for both: *"¿Qué haces?"* can mean "what do you do (job)?" **or** "what are you doing (now)?" English forces you to choose, and choosing wrong changes the meaning:

* *What **do** you **do**?* = ¿A qué te dedicas? (job)
* *What **are** you **doing**?* = ¿Qué estás haciendo? (now)

**ES:** El inglés tiene dos tiempos "presentes," y responden preguntas diferentes: el **presente simple** (FS-2) responde *"¿qué es generalmente verdad?"* — hábitos, rutinas, hechos. El **presente continuo** responde *"¿qué está pasando ahora mismo / temporalmente?"* El español muchas veces usa el presente simple para ambos: *"¿Qué haces?"* puede ser "¿a qué te dedicas?" **o** "¿qué estás haciendo ahora?" El inglés te obliga a elegir, y elegir mal cambia el significado (mira los dos ejemplos arriba).

## Part 2 · The form: a two-part engine / La forma: un motor de dos partes

**EN:** The continuous engine has **two words**: a form of ***be*** (the helper, carrying person and tense) + the main verb in **-ing** (carrying the meaning). Both parts are required. This is your first real two-part engine from FS-0 Part 3.

**ES:** El motor del continuo tiene **dos palabras**: una forma de ***be*** (el auxiliar, que lleva la persona y el tiempo) + el verbo principal en **-ing** (que lleva el significado). Las dos partes son obligatorias. Es tu primer motor de dos partes real de FS-0 Parte 3.

| SUBJECT | HELPER (be) | MAIN VERB -ing | rest | Español |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I | am | studying | English now. | Estoy estudiando inglés ahora. |
| She | is | cooking | dinner. | Está cocinando la cena. |
| They | are | watching | a movie. | Están viendo una película. |

-ing spelling / Ortografía del -ing: most verbs **+ing** (eat → eating) · silent -e drops (make → mak**ing**, write → writ**ing**) · short verb ending consonant-vowel-consonant doubles (run → ru**nn**ing, stop → sto**pp**ing) · -y keeps (study → study**ing**).

⚠️ Missing helper = broken engine / Auxiliar ausente = motor roto: ✗ *She cooking dinner.* → ✅ *She **is** cooking dinner.* This is a top-3 learner error.

## Part 3 · Negatives and questions: it's a *be* sentence! / Negaciones y preguntas: ¡es una oración con *be*!

**EN:** Great news: because the helper is *be*, the continuous uses the **FS-1 machine**, not the do-machine. Add *not* after *be*; flip *be* for questions. **Never** use do/does here.

**ES:** Buena noticia: como el auxiliar es *be*, el continuo usa la **máquina de FS-1**, no la máquina *do*. Añade *not* después de *be*; invierte *be* para preguntar. **Nunca** uses do/does aquí.

| | Example | Español |
|---|---|---|
| negative | She **isn't cooking**. / They **aren't watching** TV. | No está cocinando. / No están viendo TV. |
| question | **Is she cooking**? **Are you studying**? | ¿Está cocinando? ¿Estás estudiando? |
| short answer | Yes, she **is**. / No, she **isn't**. | Sí. / No. |

✗ *Does she cooking?* ✗ *She doesn't cooking.* — the do-machine and the -ing engine never mix.

### Mini-practice 1: put in continuous / pon en continuo: 1. I (read) ___ a good book these days. 2. (you / listen) ___ to me? 3. He (not / work) ___ today.

## Part 4 · Choosing between the two presents / Elegir entre los dos presentes

| Question to ask / Pregunta clave | Tense | Signal words / Palabras señal | Example pair |
|---|---|---|---|
| Generally true? Habit? / ¿Generalmente verdad? ¿Hábito? | simple present | always, usually, never, every day | I **drink** coffee every morning. |
| Happening now / around now / temporary? / ¿Pasando ahora / temporal? | present continuous | now, right now, at the moment, today, this week | I **am drinking** tea right now. |

Contrast pairs / Pares de contraste:

* *She **works** in a hospital.* (her job) — *She **is working** from home this week.* (temporary)
* *I **live** in Arlington.* (permanent) — *I **am living** with my parents this month.* (temporary)
* *What **do** you **do**?* (profession) — *What **are** you **doing**?* (right now)

## Part 5 · Verbs that refuse the continuous / Verbos que rechazan el continuo

**EN:** Some verbs describe **states of mind**, not actions — and states don't "happen," so they normally stay in the simple present even when talking about now. Learn the short list:

> **like, love, hate, want, need, know, understand, believe, remember, prefer, mean**

* ✗ *I am wanting a coffee.* → ✅ *I **want** a coffee.* (right now, but still simple!)
* ✗ *She is knowing the answer.* → ✅ *She **knows** the answer.*

**ES:** Algunos verbos describen **estados mentales**, no acciones — y los estados no "están pasando," así que normalmente se quedan en presente simple incluso hablando de ahora. Aprende la lista corta de arriba. *Quiero un café ahora* → *I **want** a coffee* (¡simple, aunque sea ahora!).

## Part 6 · The three jobs of -ing, united / Los tres trabajos de -ing, unidos

**EN:** You now know all the jobs of the -ing form. The word is identical; the **seat** tells you which job it's doing:

| Job | Where it sits | Example |
|---|---|---|
| continuous tense | in the VERB seat, after *be* | She **is swimming** right now. |
| gerund (FS-X) | in a NOUN seat (subject/object) or after a preposition | **Swimming** is fun. / I enjoy **swimming**. / good at **swimming** |

The two-question test from the gerunds sheet still decides every case: *be* before it + action in progress → continuous; replaceable by a noun → gerund.

**ES:** Ya conoces todos los trabajos de la forma -ing. La palabra es idéntica; el **asiento** te dice qué trabajo hace (tabla arriba). La prueba de las dos preguntas de la hoja de gerunds sigue decidiendo cada caso: ¿*be* antes + acción en progreso? → continuo; ¿reemplazable por un sustantivo? → gerund.

## Part 7 · Common mistakes / Errores comunes

| ❌ | ✅ | Why / Por qué |
|---|---|---|
| She cooking dinner. | She **is** cooking dinner. | the helper *be* is required / el auxiliar es obligatorio |
| I am go to work now. | I am **going** to work now. | after *be*, the main verb needs -ing |
| Does she sleeping? | **Is** she sleeping? | continuous questions flip *be*, never *do* |
| I am wanting a coffee. | I **want** a coffee. | *want* is a state verb / verbo de estado |
| What do you doing? | What **are** you doing? | mixed machines / máquinas mezcladas |
| I am study English every day. | I **study** English every day. | habit → simple present, no *be* |
