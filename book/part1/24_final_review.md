### A letter to Lucía, one year from now

> *On the last night of the course, Mr. Miller gave his students an envelope (un sobre). "Write a letter to yourself," he said. "Open it next September." This is Lucía's letter.*
>
> Dear Lucía,
>
> Tonight was the last night of your English course. You got your certificate, and Mateo clapped (aplaudió) louder than everyone in the room.
>
> Do you remember your first class? You were very nervous, and you only knew a few words. You wrote five short sentences about yourself, and it took you one hour. Chicago was big and cold, and you missed Cali every day. But you didn't stop. First, you worked at the Blue Door Café. Then, you got a job at the Lakeview Hotel. You met Mrs. Park, Ana and Sam, and now they are your best friends here.
>
> Your life is busier now, but it is better. You speak English at work every day, you write emails, and you understand the news on TV. Mateo still corrects your English, but not as often as last year!
>
> What are you going to do this year? Here is the plan. First, you are going to start the next course, level B1, in October. Second, you are going to read one short book in English every month. Finally, you want to be the new front desk supervisor. Ms. Rivera says you have a good chance.
>
> Here is some advice from me. You should speak English with your colleagues, even when you are tired. You shouldn't be afraid of mistakes because mistakes are how you learn. Reading before bed is a good habit, so don't stop. And you must call Mom every Sunday!
>
> I think next year will be the best year of your life. Maybe you will visit Cali with Mateo, or maybe Carlos will come to Chicago again. Maybe your English will be as good as Mateo's. (Maybe not!)
>
> When you read this letter, please remember one thing: you came here with two suitcases and no English. Be proud of that.
>
> Love,\
> Lucía (one year ago)

## D · Writing / Escritura

### Model / Modelo

> *Ana writes about her year.*
>
> This was a very important year for my English. A year ago, my first text had five short sentences, and every sentence had the same verb: *is*. Today my sentences are longer, and they are joined with *and*, *but* and *because*. First, I understand my patients better now. When they talk fast, I don't panic. Second, I write my blog in English every week, and people from other countries read it. In addition, I made new friends in class. Lucía is the best study partner in the world! My English is not perfect. For example, I still forget the *-s* in *she works*. However, I am not afraid of mistakes now. Next year, I am going to take the B1 course with Lucía. For these reasons, I think this was one of the best years of my life.
