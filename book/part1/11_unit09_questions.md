## A · Grammar / Gramática

**EN:** You already make questions in three ways: with *be* (*Is she tired?*, Unit 2), with *do/does* (*Do you like coffee?*, Unit 5) and with *be* + *-ing* (*Are you working?*, Unit 8). Here is the secret: ★ **it was always the same machine.** In this unit you see that machine, and you learn all the question words.

**ES:** Ya haces preguntas de tres maneras: con *be* (*Is she tired?*, Unidad 2), con *do/does* (*Do you like coffee?*, Unidad 5) y con *be* + *-ing* (*Are you working?*, Unidad 8). Este es el secreto: ★ **siempre fue la misma máquina.** En esta unidad ves esa máquina y aprendes todas las palabras interrogativas.

### A1 · One formula for every question: QUASM / Una fórmula para toda pregunta: QUASM

**EN:** Every English question follows one word order. Remember it as **QUASM**:

> **QU**estion word (optional) + **A**uxiliary (helper) + **S**ubject + **M**ain verb + rest
>
> *Where* + *does* + *Lucía* + *work* + ?

The engine of the sentence **splits**: the helper jumps **before** the subject, and the main verb stays **after** it.

**ES:** Toda pregunta en inglés sigue un solo orden de palabras. Recuérdalo como **QUASM**: palabra interrogativa (**QU**, opcional) + **A**uxiliar + **S**ujeto + verbo principal (**M**ain) + resto. El motor de la oración **se parte**: el auxiliar salta **antes** del sujeto, y el verbo principal se queda **después**.

| QU | A | S | M | rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Where | does | Lucía | work | ? |
| What | are | you | doing | ? |
| — | Do | you | like | coffee? |
| How many languages | can | you | speak | ? |
| Where | is | the front desk | — | ? |

**EN:** The last row has no main verb: when *be* is the only verb (Unit 2), *be* sits in the A slot alone.

**ES:** La última fila no tiene verbo principal: cuando *be* es el único verbo (Unidad 2), *be* se sienta solo en la casilla A.

**⚠ Spanish trap: the voice is not enough / Trampa del español: la entonación no basta**

**EN:** In Spanish, a question can have the same words as a statement — only the voice goes up: *¿Tú trabajas aquí?* And the subject can go at the end: *¿Dónde trabaja Lucía?* In English, the voice is not enough. You need the machine: helper **before** the subject, main verb **after** the subject. ✗ *You work here?* → ✓ ***Do** you work here?* · ✗ *Where works Lucía?* → ✓ *Where **does** Lucía **work**?*

**ES:** En español, una pregunta puede tener las mismas palabras que una afirmación — solo sube la voz: *¿Tú trabajas aquí?* Y el sujeto puede ir al final: *¿Dónde trabaja Lucía?* En inglés, la voz no basta. Necesitas la máquina: el auxiliar **antes** del sujeto, el verbo principal **después** del sujeto. ✗ *You work here?* → ✓ ***Do** you work here?* · ✗ *Where works Lucía?* → ✓ *Where **does** Lucía **work**?*

### A2 · Choosing the A slot / Elegir la casilla A

**EN:** The only decision is **which helper goes in the A slot**. You already know the answer: it is the helper of the sentence.

**ES:** La única decisión es **qué auxiliar va en la casilla A**. Ya sabes la respuesta: es el auxiliar de la oración.

| Your sentence uses… | Helper for the question | Example |
|---|---|---|
| *be* alone (Unit 2) | flip **am / is / are** | **Is** she the manager? |
| simple present (Unit 5) | hire **do / does** (verb → base) | **Does** she work here? |
| *be* + -ing (Unit 8) | flip the **be** | **Are** you listening? |
| *can* (more in Unit 13) | flip **can** | **Can** you work on Saturdays? |
| *later:* past, future (Units 10–12) | **did** / **will** | **Did** you sleep well? **Will** you come? |

**EN:** Notice the deep pattern. ★ If the sentence **already has a helper** (*be*, *can*), you just **flip** it. Only when there is **no helper** (the simple present) do you **hire** *do/does* — and *does* takes the *-s*, so the main verb goes back to its base form: *She work**s*** → *Do**es** she **work**?* In Units 10–11 the same machine hires ***did*** for the past, and in Unit 12 it flips ***will***. Same machine, new helpers.

**ES:** Fíjate en el patrón profundo. ★ Si la oración **ya tiene un auxiliar** (*be*, *can*), solo lo **inviertes**. Solo cuando **no hay auxiliar** (el presente simple) **contratas** a *do/does* — y *does* se lleva la *-s*, así que el verbo principal vuelve a su forma base: *She work**s*** → *Do**es** she **work**?* En las Unidades 10–11 la misma máquina contrata a ***did*** para el pasado, y en la Unidad 12 invierte ***will***. La misma máquina, auxiliares nuevos.

**Short answers use the same helper / Las respuestas cortas usan el mismo auxiliar**

| Question | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|
| Is she the manager? | Yes, she **is**. | No, she **isn't**. |
| Do you speak Spanish? | Yes, I **do**. | No, I **don't**. |
| Are you working now? | Yes, I **am**. | No, I**'m not**. |
| Can you work on Saturdays? | Yes, I **can**. | No, I **can't**. |

### A3 · The QU slot: question words / La casilla QU: las palabras interrogativas

**EN:** The QU slot holds the question word. It asks for information. Yes/No questions leave the QU slot empty: *(—) Do you work here?*

**ES:** La casilla QU lleva la palabra interrogativa. Pide información. Las preguntas de sí/no dejan la casilla QU vacía: *(—) Do you work here?*

| Word | Asks about / Pregunta por | Example | Español |
|---|---|---|---|
| **what** | thing / cosa | What do you want? | ¿qué? ¿cuál? |
| **where** | place / lugar | Where does she live? | ¿dónde? |
| **when** | time / tiempo | When is your interview? | ¿cuándo? |
| **what time** | the hour / la hora | What time does the shift start? | ¿a qué hora? |
| **who** | person / persona | Who is your manager? | ¿quién? |
| **why** | reason / razón | Why do you want this job? | ¿por qué? |
| **how** | manner / manera | How do you spell that? | ¿cómo? |
| **how old** | age / edad | How old is Mateo? | ¿cuántos años? |
| **how many** | number (count) / número | How many hours do you work? | ¿cuántos/as? |
| **how much** | amount, price / cantidad, precio | How much is the pay? | ¿cuánto? |
| **how often** | frequency (Unit 7) / frecuencia | How often do you work on Saturdays? | ¿con qué frecuencia? |
| **how long** | length of time / duración | How long is the shift? | ¿cuánto dura? |
| **which** | choice / elección | Which shift do you want? | ¿cuál? |
| **whose** | owner (Unit 4) / dueño | Whose coat is this? | ¿de quién? |

**⚠ Three Spanish traps / Tres trampas del español**

**EN:**

* ***¿Cuál es tu nombre?*** → ✓ ***What** is your name?* (✗ *Which is your name?*). Use *which* only for a choice from a small group: *Which shift — morning or afternoon?*
* ***¿Cuántos años tienes?*** → ✓ ***How old** are you?* (✗ *How many years do you have?* — the *tener* trap, Unit 2).
* ***how much / how many*** → *many* for count nouns, *much* for noncount nouns (Unit 3): *How **many** customers? How **much** money?*

**ES:** *¿Cuál es tu nombre?* → ✓ *What is your name?* (✗ *Which is your name?*). Usa *which* solo para elegir de un grupo pequeño: *Which shift — morning or afternoon?* (¿Qué turno — mañana o tarde?). *¿Cuántos años tienes?* → ✓ *How old are you?* (✗ *How many years do you have?* — la trampa de *tener*, Unidad 2). *How much / how many*: *many* para sustantivos contables, *much* para incontables (Unidad 3).

**EN:** And remember: ***Why…?*** asks, ***Because…*** answers. *Why do you want this job? — **Because** I like people.*

**ES:** Y recuerda: ***Why…?*** pregunta, ***Because…*** responde. *¿Por qué quieres este trabajo? — Porque me gusta la gente.*

**Mini-practice 2 / Mini-práctica 2** — Write the question word. / Escribe la palabra interrogativa.

1. ___ do you live? — On Maple Street.
2. ___ is your teacher? — Mr. Miller.
3. ___ is Mrs. Park? — She's 72.
4. ___ do you study English? — Because I need it for my job.
5. ___ is the coffee? — It's $3.

### A4 · The exception: subject questions / La excepción: preguntas de sujeto

**EN:** One special case. Sometimes the question word **is the subject**: you ask *who does the action*. Then there is nothing to flip and **no helper**. The question word sits in the subject seat, and the sentence keeps normal order — with the *-s* of he/she/it.

**ES:** Un caso especial. A veces la palabra interrogativa **es el sujeto**: preguntas *quién hace la acción*. Entonces no hay nada que invertir y **no hay auxiliar**. La palabra interrogativa se sienta en el asiento del sujeto, y la oración mantiene el orden normal — con la *-s* de he/she/it.

| Question | *Who* is the… | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **Who works** at the front desk? | subject | *Lucía* works at the front desk. |
| **Who lives** here? | subject | *Lucía and Mateo* live here. |
| **What happens** on the first day? | subject | *A coworker* shows you everything. |
| Who **does** Mateo **call** on Sundays? | object | He calls *his dad*. |

**EN:** ★ The test: put the answer in the place of *who* or *what*. If the answer sits in the **subject** seat (*Lucía works…*), it is a subject question — no helper. ✗ *Who does work here?* → ✓ *Who **works** here?* If the answer sits in the **object** seat (*He calls his dad*), use QUASM as usual.

**ES:** ★ La prueba: pon la respuesta en el lugar de *who* o *what*. Si la respuesta se sienta en el asiento del **sujeto** (*Lucía works…*), es una pregunta de sujeto — sin auxiliar. ✗ *Who does work here?* → ✓ *Who **works** here?* Si la respuesta se sienta en el asiento del **objeto** (*He calls his dad*), usa QUASM como siempre.

**Mini-practice 3 / Mini-práctica 3** — Ask about the **bold** words. / Pregunta por las palabras en **negrita**.

1. **Mrs. Park** has a cat.
2. **Sam** makes the coffee.
3. Lucía calls **her mother** on Sundays.

### A5 · Common mistakes / Errores comunes

| ✗ | ✓ | Why / Por qué |
|---|---|---|
| Where she lives? | Where **does she live**? | no helper hired |
| What you are doing? | What **are you** doing? | flip the *be*: A before S |
| Why she isn't here? | Why **isn't she** here? | helper before subject, always |
| Does she works here? | Does she **work** here? | *does* takes the -s |
| Who does work here? | Who **works** here? | subject question: no helper |
| How much books do you have? | How **many** books…? | count noun → *many* |
| You can help me? (written) | **Can you** help me? | flip the helper |
| What means "shift"? | What **does** "shift" **mean**? | QUASM applies! |
| How many years do you have? | How **old** are you? | age → *be*, not *have* |
| Which is your name? | **What** is your name? | *¿cuál?* is usually *what* |

### Word tip / Consejo de palabras

**EN:** Three false friends that appear in job interviews. Don't say them with the Spanish meaning!

| Spanish | ✗ Not | ✓ Say |
|---|---|---|

### The interview

> **Ms. Rivera:** Good afternoon. I'm Laura Rivera, the manager. Please sit down.
>
> **Lucía:** Good afternoon. Nice to meet you.
>
> **Ms. Rivera:** So, Lucía, where do you work now?
>
> **Lucía:** I work at the Blue Door Café, from seven to three. I talk to customers all day.
>
> **Ms. Rivera:** Why do you want this job?
>
> **Lucía:** Because I like people, and I speak two languages. Many of your guests speak Spanish.
>
> **Ms. Rivera:** That's true. Can you use a computer?
>
> **Lucía:** Yes, I can. I'm good at computers, and I learn fast.
>
> **Ms. Rivera:** Are you studying now?
>
> **Lucía:** Yes, I am. I'm studying English at Lincoln Adult School on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
>
> **Ms. Rivera:** Good. The shift is from eight to four, Monday to Friday. How often can you work on Saturdays?
>
> **Lucía:** Two Saturdays a month is fine. Can I ask some questions? How much is the pay?
>
> **Ms. Rivera:** It's nineteen dollars an hour.
>
> **Lucía:** And what happens on the first day?
>
> **Ms. Rivera:** A coworker shows you everything.
>
> **Lucía:** Who works at the front desk in the morning?
>
> **Ms. Rivera:** Three receptionists. They are very nice. Well, Lucía, you are friendly and hard-working. When can you start?
>
> **Lucía:** Really? In two weeks!
>
> **Ms. Rivera:** Welcome to the Lakeview Hotel!

### Checklist / Lista de control

- ☐ Every question ends with a question mark (?).
- ☐ In every question, the helper comes **before** the subject (QUASM).
- ☐ After *do/does*, the main verb is in the base form (*Does she work…?*, not *works*).
- ☐ I use at least 3 helpers: *is/are*, *do/does*, *can*.
- ☐ I use at least 4 question words.
- ☐ Short answers use the same helper as the question (*Can you…? — Yes, I can.*).
- ☐ Every answer has a subject.
