### Mateo's school trip

*by Mateo Ramírez, Grade 4, Hawthorne Elementary School*

> Last Wednesday my class went to the Field Museum. We left school at nine o'clock and went by bus. Leo and I were next to the window. The trip took forty minutes. On the bus, Ms. Johnson told us the museum rules: "Walk. Don't run. Don't touch."
>
> First we saw Sue. Sue is a Tyrannosaurus rex, and it is very, very big — 40 feet (12 meters) long! Sue lived 67 million years ago. I knew a lot of facts about T. rex, and I told my friends. Leo said, "Mateo, you are a dinosaur teacher!" Ms. Johnson laughed.
>
> Then we visited the rooms about Ancient Egypt, and we saw real mummies (momias). They were a little scary (daban miedo). At twelve we ate our lunch in a big room. Mom made me an arepa with cheese. I didn't eat my apple. Sorry, Mom!
>
> After lunch we went to the gift shop (tienda de regalos). I had five dollars from Grandma Rosa, and I bought a small T. rex. I paid with my own money!
>
> Next month we're going to visit the zoo. I'm going to see the lions and the gorillas, and I'm going to write a report about them. Leo's mom is coming with us. My mom can't come because she works, but she says she will help me with my report. I think the zoo will be fun, but nothing will be like Sue!
>
> P.S. Last night Mom said, "I goed to a museum in Cali." No, Mom! You WENT!

## D · Writing / Escritura

### Model / Modelo

> Last month was a lot of fun. On a Saturday night, Ana and I went to a salsa concert downtown. We took the train after dinner. The music was fantastic, and we danced for three hours! I didn't know many songs, but Ana knew all of them. After the concert we ate tacos, and I got home at midnight. Next month is going to be quiet. I'm going to study for my English test. On May 16, Mrs. Park and I are taking a Korean cooking class. I think it will be fun — and delicious! — *Lucía*

### Your turn / Tu turno

**EN:** Write a short story (**10 sentences**) in two parts: something you did **last month**, and something you are going to do **next month**.
**ES:** Escribe una historia corta (**10 oraciones**) en dos partes: algo que hiciste **el mes pasado** y algo que vas a hacer **el próximo mes**.

1. `Last month + was + ADJECTIVE.`
2. `On DAY, + I + PAST VERB + ...`
3. `First, + I / we + PAST VERB + ...`
4. `Then + I / we + PAST VERB + ...`
5. `I + didn't + BASE VERB + ..., but + ...`
6. `After that, + I / we + PAST VERB + ...`
7. `Next month + I'm going to + VERB + ...`
8. `On DATE, + I'm + VERB-ing + ...` (arrangement)
9. `I think + it + will + be + ADJECTIVE.`
10. `I'm not going to + VERB + ...`

*Ideas en español, oraciones en inglés; si no puedes etiquetar S / V / O en tu oración, reconstrúyela.*

### Checklist / Lista de control

- ☐ Every sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop (.). No empty subject seat.
- ☐ Part 1 (past): at least **3 irregular** and **2 regular** past verbs, and *was* or *were* once.
- ☐ I used *didn't* + **base verb** once (✗ *didn't went*).
- ☐ Part 2 (future): *going to* at least **2 times**, *will* at least **once**, and the present continuous for one arrangement.
- ☐ Time words are correct: *last month, next month, on May 16* (✗ *the last month*, ✗ *the next month*).
