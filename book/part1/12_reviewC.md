2. What do you know about Lucía? Write three things. / ¿Qué sabes de Lucía? Escribe tres cosas.

### Ana's blog: My friend Lucía

> Hello, readers! Today I'm writing about my friend Lucía. We are classmates at Lincoln Adult School.
>
> Lucía is from Cali, in Colombia. She is 32, and she lives in Chicago with her son, Mateo. She has curly dark hair, like her brother Carlos, and a big smile. She is a very hard-working person. She is always on time for class, and she never forgets her notebook. She is also really funny. She sometimes tells jokes in Spanish, and Mr. Miller always says, "In English, please!"
>
> Lucía often cooks Colombian food for her friends. Her neighbor, Mrs. Park, loves Lucía's soup. On Sundays, Lucía and Mateo usually walk by the lake — but not in December! Lucía doesn't like cold weather. Right now it's snowing, and she is wearing two sweaters in class.
>
> These days, Lucía's life is changing. She has a new job! This is her first week at the front desk of the Lakeview Hotel. She is learning a new computer system, and she is meeting a lot of people. "The guests are friendly," she says, "but the phone never stops!" Mateo is very proud (orgulloso) of his mom. He tells everyone, "My mom works at a big hotel!"
>
> Now I have some questions for you, readers. Do you have a friend like Lucía? What is your friend like? How often do you see your friends? Write in the comments (comentarios)!

## D · Writing / Escritura

### Your turn / Tu turno

**EN:** Write a **profile of a friend** in **8–9 sentences**, like Ana's blog: appearance, personality, habits, and what your friend is doing these days. Then write **3 questions** you want to ask this friend.

**ES:** Escribe un **perfil de un amigo o amiga** en **8–9 oraciones**, como el blog de Ana: apariencia, personalidad, hábitos y lo que tu amigo/a está haciendo estos días. Luego escribe **3 preguntas** que quieres hacerle.

1. `NAME + is + my + friend / neighbor / coworker.`
2. `He/She + is + from + PLACE, and + he/she + is + AGE.`
3. `He/She + has + ADJ + ADJ + hair + and + …`
4. `He/She + is + a + very / really + ADJ + person.`
5. `He/She + always / usually / often + VERB-s + …`
6. `He/She + is + never + ADJ + …`
7. `He/She + VERB-s + … + once a week / every day.`
8. `These days, he/she + is + VERB-ing + …`
9. `Right now, he/she + is + VERB-ing + …`

Questions / Preguntas: `QU + A + S + M + …?` — for example: `What + are + you + doing + these days?` · `How often + do + you + …?` · `Can + you + …?`

*Ideas en español, oraciones en inglés; si no puedes etiquetar S / V / O en tu oración, reconstrúyela.*

### Checklist / Lista de control

- ☐ Every sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop (.).
- ☐ At least 4 adjectives before a noun, with no *-s*.
- ☐ At least 3 frequency adverbs: after *be*, before other verbs.
- ☐ At least 2 sentences in the present continuous (*these days, right now*), each with *am/is/are*.
- ☐ Habits in the simple present, with the *-s* for he/she (Unit 5).
- ☐ 3 questions with QUASM: helper before the subject.
- ☐ I can label S, V, O or D in every sentence.
