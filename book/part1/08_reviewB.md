### Mateo's Week

*My Week — by Mateo Ramírez, Grade 4, Ms. Johnson's class*

> My name is Mateo Ramírez. I am nine, and I live on Maple Street in Chicago with my mom. Her name is Lucía. She works at the Blue Door Café, and at night she studies English. I help her with her homework! My dad, Andrés, lives in Cali, in Colombia. I talk to him on a video call on Sundays. My grandmother Rosa and my uncle Carlos live in Cali too. Carlos is an engineer, and he is my hero.
>
> On school days, I get up at a quarter past six. I don't like mornings! At a quarter to seven, I go to Mrs. Park's apartment. She is our neighbor, and she has a cat. Its name is Tofu. Mrs. Park walks with me to school. My school is Hawthorne Elementary, and classes start at eight. My best friend, Leo, is in my class. We play soccer at lunch.
>
> On Tuesdays and Thursdays, Mom has class in the evening, and I stay with Mrs. Park. Does she help me with my homework? Yes, she does. She knows a lot about math!
>
> On Saturday mornings, Leo and I play in the park at the end of our street. Then I go to the library on Park Avenue. I read books about dinosaurs there. My favorite dinosaur is the T. rex.
>
> My week is busy, but I like it. And a secret: I correct my mom's English. Shh — don't tell her!

## D · Writing / Escritura

### Your turn / Tu turno

**EN:** Write **8–9 sentences**: *A week in my life*. Write about the people in your home, your day, and your neighborhood. Use Mateo's text as a model.
**ES:** Escribe **8–9 oraciones**: *Una semana en mi vida*. Escribe sobre las personas de tu casa, tu día y tu barrio. Usa el texto de Mateo como modelo.

1. `I + live + on + STREET + in + CITY + with + my + PERSON.`
2. `His / Her + name + is + NAME.`
3. `He / She + VERB-s + at / in + PLACE.`
4. `On + school days / work days, I + get up + at + TIME.`
5. `I + don't + VERB + REST.`
6. `On + DAY(S), I + VERB + in the morning / at night.`
7. `There is / There are + PLACE(S) + near + my home.`
8. `On the weekend, I + go to + PLACE + and + VERB.`
9. `My week + is + ADJECTIVE, but I + like / love + it.`

*Ideas en español, oraciones en inglés; si no puedes etiquetar S / V / O en tu oración, reconstrúyela.*

### Checklist / Lista de control

- ☐ Every sentence starts with a capital letter and ends with a full stop (.).
- ☐ No empty subject seat: every sentence has a subject.
- ☐ I use *his / her / my* and one 's. *(Unit 4)*
- ☐ Every *he / she* verb has the -s, and I use *don't / doesn't* one time or more. *(Unit 5)*
- ☐ I use *in, on* and *at* for places and times. *(Unit 6)*
