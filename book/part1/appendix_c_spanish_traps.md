# Appendix C · Spanish Traps: Quick Index / Trampas del español: índice rápido

**EN:** The mistakes Spanish speakers make most often, all in one place. Before you give your writing to anyone, read this page and check your text for each trap.

**ES:** Los errores más frecuentes de los hispanohablantes, todos en un lugar. Antes de entregarle tu texto a alguien, lee esta página y revisa tu texto buscando cada trampa.

## Sentence building / Construir la oración

| Spanish habit / Hábito del español | ✗ | ✓ | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| empty subject (*Es tarde*) | Is late. | **It** is late. | 1 |
| *no* + verb | He no works. | He **doesn't** work. | 5 |
| question by intonation | You like coffee? | **Do you** like coffee? | 5, 9 |
| adjective after the noun | a car red | a **red car** | 7 |
| plural adjective | beautifuls flowers | **beautiful** flowers | 7 |
| *más grande* | more big | **bigger** | 16 |
| *que* in comparisons | bigger that | bigger **than** | 16 |
| comma between two sentences | I was tired, I went home. | I was tired, **so** I went home. | 17 |

## Verbs / Verbos

| Spanish habit / Hábito del español | ✗ | ✓ | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| *tener* for age | I have 30 years. | I **am** 30. | 2 |
| *tener* hambre / frío / razón | I have hunger. | I **am** hungry. | 2 |
| *hay* | Have a park here. | **There is** a park here. | 3 |
| no -s for he/she/it | She work here. | She work**s** here. | 5 |
| double -s | Does she works? | Does she **work**? | 5 |
| present for "now" | I cook now. | I**'m cooking** now. | 8 |
| continuous with *know / want* | I'm knowing. | I **know**. | 8 |
| past after *did* | Did you went? | Did you **go**? | 11 |
| *ir a* + infinitive | I go to travel. | I**'m going to** travel. | 12 |
| modal + *to* | I can to swim. | I can **swim**. | 13 |
| modal with *do* | I don't can. | I **can't**. | 13 |
| *para* + infinitive | for learn | **to** learn | 15 |
| infinitive as subject | Swim is fun. | **Swimming** is fun. | 15 |
| after a preposition | before go | before **going** | 6, 15 |

## Nouns, articles and pronouns / Sustantivos, artículos y pronombres

| Spanish habit / Hábito del español | ✗ | ✓ | Unit |
|---|---|---|---|
| no article with jobs | She is doctor. | She is **a** doctor. | 2, 3 |
| *el/la* for general ideas | The life is hard. | **Life** is hard. | 3 |
| countable in Spanish | informations, advices | **information**, **advice** | 3 |
| *las tareas* | homeworks | **homework** / assignments | 3 |
| *personas* | persons | **people** | 3 |
| *su* = his/her/their | Lucía loves his son. | Lucía loves **her** son. | 4 |
| *el libro de María* | the book of María | **María's** book | 4 |
| *yo y mi amigo* | Me and my friend went… | **My friend and I** went… | 4 |
