## Unit 1 · Hello! / ¡Hola! — Week 1 · A1
- **Grammar:** A1 word families (noun, verb, adjective, pronoun, preposition, article) · A2 the seats (Pattern A action verb, Pattern B *be*), golden rule (noun seats), subject seat never empty · A3 helper verbs — recognize only · A4 classroom instructions (imperatives: *Read. Write. Listen. Don't use a dictionary.*) · A5 classroom glossary · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** greetings & goodbyes, *How are you?* answers, the alphabet (letter names that confuse Spanish speakers: A, E, I, G, J, R, Y), numbers 0–20, classroom objects.
- **Reading:** "Hello, I'm Lucía" — Lucía introduces herself on the first night at Lincoln Adult School (≈90 words).
- **Writing:** 5 sentences introducing yourself (name, spelling, country, city, one thing you like).

## Unit 2 · People and places / Personas y lugares — Week 2 · A1
- **Grammar:** *be* — why *be* is special (ser + estar), am/is/are + contractions, negatives, questions by flipping + short answers, the *tener* trap (age, hungry, cold, right, afraid) · capital letters (I, names, countries, nationalities, languages, days, months — Spanish contrast table from FS-13) · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** 8 countries + nationalities (Colombia/Colombian, Mexico/Mexican, the United States/American, Korea/Korean, Nigeria/Nigerian, Spain/Spanish, Brazil/Brazilian, China/Chinese), 8 jobs, feelings (happy, tired, hungry, nervous…), numbers 20–100.
- **Reading:** "My class at Lincoln Adult School" — Lucía describes Mr. Miller, Ana and two other classmates (country, job, age, feeling).
- **Writing:** 6 sentences: you + one classmate/friend (name, country, nationality, job, age, how you feel today) + 2 questions with *be*.

## Unit 3 · My new home / Mi nueva casa — Week 3 · A1
- **Grammar:** plurals (rules + irregulars), count vs. noncount (the Spanish-countable trap list), a/an/the/nothing (a/an by sound; general statements take nothing), *there is / there are* (= hay; negatives, questions), a lot of / some basic · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** rooms, furniture, kitchen/household objects, *upstairs, next to, big/small, old/new*.
- **Reading:** "The apartment on Maple Street" — the 2-bedroom apartment, 3rd floor, park and bus stop nearby; there is/are-heavy.
- **Writing:** describe your home (6 sentences, ≥3 *there is/are*, ≥1 plural irregular or noncount noun).

## Review A · Units 1–3 — Week 4
- **Reading:** "A message to Mom" — Lucía writes to Rosa about the apartment, the class, the café (≈150 words).
- **Writing:** a message to your family or a friend about your home and your English class (7–8 sentences).

## Unit 4 · Family and friends / Familia y amigos — Week 5 · A1
- **Grammar:** pronouns change shape by seat (subject vs. object; after prepositions), possessive + noun vs. possessive alone, the *su* problem, his/her follows the owner, 's (owner first; parents'), its vs. it's, **this/that/these/those** (near/far × singular/plural — new) · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** family members (incl. grandparents, cousin, aunt, uncle, nephew/niece, husband/wife, only child), basic appearance (tall, short, hair: long/short/curly, eyes).
- **Reading:** "Photos on the fridge" — Lucía shows Mrs. Park the family photos; Carlos, Rosa, Mateo's father (lives in Cali; separated — keep neutral).
- **Writing:** describe 3 people in your family with pronouns and possessives (7 sentences).

## Unit 5 · My day / Mi día — Week 6 · A1
- **Grammar:** what the simple present is for (not "now"), the -s for he/she/it + spelling, do/does carries the grammar (the -s jumps to the helper), negatives, questions, short answers, *be* vs. normal verbs (the fork), mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** routine verbs (wake up, get up, take a shower, have breakfast, go to work, start, finish, come home, cook, go to bed), telling the time (o'clock, half past, quarter to/past, a.m./p.m.).
- **Reading:** "A day in Lucía's life" — café shift 7–3, Mateo's school, class Tue/Thu.
- **Writing:** your daily routine (8 sentences, times, ≥2 negatives), + 3 sentences about another person's day (he/she -s).

## Unit 6 · Around town / Por la ciudad — Week 7 · A1–A2
- **Grammar:** in/on/at — place (in = inside/city/country, on = surface/street, at = point/address/work/home), time (at 7, on Monday, in May/2025/the morning; at night, on the weekend US), no preposition with *next/last/every/tomorrow*, preposition + -ing · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** places in town (bank, pharmacy, post office, supermarket, library, hospital, bus stop, train station…), days, months, ordinal numbers & dates (*on September 5th*).
- **Reading:** "Our neighborhood" — Maple Street, the park, the library where Mateo reads about dinosaurs, the L train.
- **Writing:** your neighborhood + your week's timetable (8 sentences, in/on/at each ≥2).

## Review B · Units 4–6 — Week 8
- **Reading:** "Mateo's week" — Mateo's own text for school about his family, his day, and his neighborhood (≈200 words).
- **Writing:** a week in your life (8–9 sentences).

## Unit 7 · Describing people / Describir personas — Week 9 · A2
- **Grammar (new):** adjectives never take plural -s, adjective **before** the noun (a tall man, not a man tall), be + adjective, *very / really / quite / a bit*, adjective order light (size before color), adverbs of frequency (always 100% → never 0%) — position: **after *be***, **before other verbs**, *How often…? — every day / once a week / twice a month*; *-ed/-ing* feelings **not** here (Part 2).
- **Vocabulary:** appearance, personality (kind, funny, shy, friendly, patient, lazy, hard-working, quiet, talkative, serious), frequency expressions.
- **Reading:** "My neighbor, Mrs. Park" — her appearance, personality, habits, Tofu the cat.
- **Writing:** describe a person you know (8 sentences, ≥4 adjectives before nouns, ≥3 frequency adverbs correctly placed).

## Unit 8 · Right now / Ahora mismo — Week 10 · A2
- **Grammar:** two presents, two meanings; be + -ing form + spelling (make→making, run→running), negatives/questions are *be* sentences, choosing between the presents (signal words), stative verbs (like, want, know, need, understand…), the three jobs of -ing (preview) · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** weather (sunny, cloudy, windy, snowing, raining, cold, freezing), seasons, clothes (coat, boots, scarf, gloves, hat, sweater, jeans…).
- **Reading:** "The first snow" — a text-message chain: Lucía at the café, Mateo in the snow with Leo, Ana, Mrs. Park.
- **Writing:** a message to a friend: where you are, what you are doing, what people around you are doing, the weather, what you usually do (7–8 sentences).

## Unit 9 · Asking questions / Hacer preguntas — Week 11 · A2
- **Grammar:** QUASM (QU-word + Auxiliary + Subject + Main verb), choosing the A slot (is/are, do/does/did, can, will), question words (what, where, when, who, why, how, how old/many/much/often/long, which, whose), subject questions (Who lives here?) · mistakes. (Only present tenses + can in examples; one "did" preview line pointing to Unit 10.)
- **Vocabulary:** work & interviews (experience, customer, shift, full-time, part-time, salary, manager, front desk, schedule, skills, *I'm good at…*).
- **Reading:** "The interview" — dialogue: Lucía's interview with the Lakeview Hotel manager, Ms. Rivera.
- **Writing:** write 8 interview questions for a job you know, and your answers.

## Review C · Units 7–9 — Week 12
- **Reading:** "Ana's blog: My friend Lucía" — Ana describes Lucía, what she's doing now (new job), and asks her readers questions (≈220 words).
- **Writing:** a profile of a friend: appearance, personality, habits, what they are doing these days + 3 questions you want to ask them.

## Unit 10 · Last weekend / El fin de semana pasado — Week 13 · A2
- **Grammar:** what the past does (finished time), regular -ed + spelling (stop→stopped, study→studied) + pronunciation /t/ /d/ /ɪd/, was/were (negatives, questions), past time words (yesterday, last…, …ago, in 2020), preview: *did* + base (full treatment Unit 11) · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** weekend activities with regular verbs (visit, watch, clean, cook, play, walk, listen, call, stay, relax, travel, dance), past time expressions.
- **Reading:** "A busy weekend" — Lucía's first weekend after starting at the hotel: cleaned, cooked for Mrs. Park, watched a movie with Mateo, was tired.
- **Writing:** your last weekend (8 sentences, ≥5 regular past, ≥2 was/were).

## Unit 11 · Life stories / Historias de vida — Week 14 · A2
- **Grammar:** irregular verbs — the 30-verb memory list (be, have, do, go, get, make, take, see, come, give, know, think, say, tell, find, leave, meet, buy, bring, eat, drink, sleep, write, read, speak, begin, feel, pay, teach, put), did-negatives and did-questions (did carries the past, main verb base), mistakes · **Reading skill:** context clues + cognates / false friends (FS-R1 Parts 3–4 simplified).
- **Vocabulary:** life events (be born, grow up, go to school, graduate, get a job, meet, get married, have a baby, move to, retire).
- **Reading:** "Mrs. Park's story" — born in Seoul 1953, teacher, came to Chicago 1975, taught math 35 years, retired, husband died 2019.
- **Writing:** a short biography of an older family member (9–10 sentences, ≥6 irregular past).

## Unit 12 · Plans / Planes — Week 15 · A2
- **Grammar:** two futures, two flavors (will = decision now / promise / prediction; going to = plan / evidence), forms (+ − ?), the three "going to" patterns (going to + verb, going to + place, going + -ing), present continuous for fixed arrangements · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** travel & transport (plane, airport, ticket, suitcase, passport, trip, by bus/train/car, on foot, hotel, beach, lake, sightseeing), time expressions (tomorrow, next week, in two weeks, soon).
- **Reading:** "Carlos is coming!" — Carlos's summer visit plans; what they're going to do; predictions about the weather.
- **Writing:** an email to a friend about your plans for next month (9–10 sentences, will + going to + present continuous).

## Review D · Units 10–12 — Week 16
- **Reading:** "Mateo's school trip" — Mateo's class went to the Field Museum (dinosaur Sue!) last week; next month they are going to the zoo (≈230 words, past + future).
- **Writing:** a story: something you did last month and something you are going to do next month.

## Unit 13 · Rules and health / Reglas y salud — Week 17 · A2
- **Grammar:** what modals do (ability, possibility, advice, obligation), the three laws (base verb after, no -s, no do-support), can/can't, could (past ability + polite), should (advice), must/mustn't, have to / don't have to (the exception: normal verb + do) — *mustn't ≠ don't have to* · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** body parts, health problems (headache, fever, sore throat, cough, cold, flu, backache), at the doctor's (appointment, medicine, prescription, rest), hotel rules.
- **Reading:** "At the doctor's" — Mateo has a fever; the doctor's advice; hotel rule sheet Lucía must follow.
- **Writing:** a message of advice to a sick friend + 4 rules of your workplace/home (9 sentences).

## Unit 14 · Food and shopping / Comida y compras — Week 18 · A2
- **Grammar:** polite machines (Could you…? Can I have…? Would you like…? I'd like…, Shall I…? / Let's…), the two traps (*Can you give me* is less polite; *I want* too direct), count/noncount revisited: some/any (+ − ?), much/many/a lot of, How much/How many, a bottle/cup/piece of · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** food, drinks, containers, shops, prices & money (*How much is it? It's $4.50*), restaurant phrases (menu, order, bill, tip).
- **Reading:** "Dinner at Mrs. Park's" + a short restaurant dialogue; Lucía brings arepas.
- **Writing:** an invitation message (requests/offers) + a shopping list with quantities (some/any/a bottle of).

## Unit 15 · Free time / Tiempo libre — Week 19 · A2
- **Grammar:** verbs in noun costumes (the seats again), gerund in subject seat, object seat: the first verb decides (like/love/enjoy/hate/finish + -ing; want/need/would like/hope/plan/decide + to), after a preposition → -ing (good at, interested in, tired of), purpose = to (*para*), go + -ing activities, the other -ing (continuous) · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** hobbies & sports (swimming, running, reading, cooking, dancing, playing soccer/the guitar, drawing, gardening, hiking, going to the movies), *be good at / be interested in*.
- **Reading:** "What do you do for fun?" — Sam's survey for his class project: Lucía, Mateo, Mrs. Park, Ana answer.
- **Writing:** your free time (9–10 sentences, ≥3 gerunds, ≥3 infinitives, ≥1 purpose *to*).

## Review E · Units 13–15 — Week 20
- **Reading:** "Learning a language as an adult" — a short magazine-style article (Mr. Miller's advice column) with advice (should/can), hobbies (-ing), food/shopping as practice (≈250 words).
- **Writing:** your opinion: what you should do to learn English faster (9–10 sentences).

## Unit 16 · Comparing places / Comparar lugares — Week 21 · A2
- **Grammar (new):** comparatives (short: -er + than; spelling big→bigger, happy→happier; long: more + adj + than), superlatives (the -est / the most), irregular good/better/best, bad/worse/worst, far/farther, *as … as / not as … as*, ⚠ ✗ *more big*, ✗ *more better*, *than* not *that*, *the* before superlatives; *than me* vs. *than I am* · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** places & nature (city, town, village, countryside, mountain, river, beach, lake, weather words), adjectives for places (crowded, quiet, safe, dangerous, expensive, cheap, modern, beautiful, noisy, clean).
- **Reading:** "Chicago or Cali?" — one year later: Lucía compares the two cities (weather, people, food, prices).
- **Writing:** compare two places you know (9–10 sentences, ≥4 comparatives, ≥2 superlatives, 1 as…as).

## Unit 17 · Joining ideas / Unir ideas — Week 22 · A2
- **Grammar:** the clause (one engine = one clause), simple sentences, compound with FANBOYS (focus: and, but, or, so) + comma, complex with because / when / if (preview only) / before / after, the three crimes (run-on, comma splice, fragment — *Because I was tired.*), subject–verb agreement (true subject, prepositional phrase blindfold, and-teams plural, *everyone/people*, there is/are boss after the verb) · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** work & study (colleague, guest, check in/out, reservation, course, exam, certificate, promotion, busy, stressful, proud), opinion words (I think, I feel, in my opinion).
- **Reading:** "Work, school and everything" — Lucía's week at the hotel and her final English course; long joined sentences.
- **Writing:** 10 joined sentences about your week (≥2 and, ≥2 but, ≥2 so, ≥2 because, 1 when).

## Unit 18 · Paragraphs and emails / Párrafos y emails — Week 23 · A2
- **Grammar/skills:** general vs. specific, the topic sentence, support sentences, the concluding sentence, connector families (adding, contrast, result, time), listing signals (first, second, next, then, finally; *first of all*, *also*, *in addition*; comma after) + Spanish traps (*in first place*, *finally* ≠ *at the end*), paragraph skeletons (story, opinion), fact vs. opinion, email frames (informal: Hi…/Love; formal: Dear…/Best regards; subject line) · mistakes.
- **Vocabulary:** email & writing language (subject, attachment, reply, Dear…, Best regards, I'm writing to…, Thank you for…, I look forward to…, Please let me know…), connectors.
- **Reading:** "One year in Chicago" — Lucía's paragraph for Mr. Miller + her formal email to the hotel manager asking for a schedule change.
- **Writing:** (1) a 100–130-word paragraph about how your English changed; (2) a short formal or informal email.

## Final review · Units 1–18 — Week 24
- **Reading:** "A letter to Lucía, one year from now" / a ~300-word text from Lucía's point of view looking back and forward; mixed questions.
- **Grammar:** a 40-item mixed test across all units (Chapter 0 tracker: /40) + score bands and "go back to Unit X, Section A_" advice per item group.
- **Writing:** a 120–150-word paragraph "My year with English" + compare with your Week-1 text.
- **A2 can-do checklist** (reading, writing, grammar, vocabulary) + "What comes next: Part 2".
