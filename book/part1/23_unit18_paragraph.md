## A · Grammar / Gramática

**EN:** In Unit 17 you joined two ideas in **one sentence**. Now you join sentences into a **paragraph**: one idea, built in layers. A paragraph is the basic unit of all writing — a school text, a report at work, a message, an email. This unit gives you its shape, the small words that connect it, and two email frames.

**ES:** En la Unidad 17 uniste dos ideas en **una oración**. Ahora unes oraciones en un **párrafo**: una idea, construida en capas. El párrafo es la unidad básica de todo lo que se escribe — un texto para la escuela, un informe en el trabajo, un mensaje, un email. Esta unidad te da su forma, las palabras pequeñas que lo conectan y dos marcos de email.

### A1 · General and specific: the shape of a paragraph / General y específico: la forma de un párrafo

**EN:** A **general** sentence covers many things. A **specific** sentence covers one thing. Each step down this ladder is more specific:

> food → fruit → mangoes → the mangoes in my mother's garden in Cali

A **paragraph** starts with its most general sentence: the **topic sentence**. Then **support sentences** prove it with specific reasons, examples or details. At the end, a **concluding sentence** says the main idea again in new words. The test question for readers: **"Which sentence covers all the others?"** That one is the topic sentence.

**ES:** Una oración **general** cubre muchas cosas. Una oración **específica** cubre una sola. Cada escalón de esta escalera es más específico:

> comida → fruta → mangos → los mangos del jardín de mi mamá en Cali

Un **párrafo** empieza con su oración más general: la **topic sentence** (oración principal). Luego, las **oraciones de apoyo** la prueban con razones, ejemplos o detalles específicos. Al final, una **oración de cierre** dice la idea principal otra vez con otras palabras. La pregunta de prueba para el lector: **"¿Qué oración cubre a todas las demás?"** Esa es la topic sentence.

| Layer / Capa | Job / Trabajo | Example / Ejemplo |
|---|---|---|
| **Topic sentence** | the general idea / la idea general | My neighborhood is great for families. |
| Support 1 | a specific reason / una razón | First, there is a big park. |
| Support 2 | a specific reason | Second, the school is near. |
| Support 3 | a specific reason | Finally, the neighbors are friendly. |
| **Concluding sentence** | the idea again / la idea otra vez | For these reasons, families love it here. |

### A2 · Connector families / Familias de conectores

**EN:** Connectors are the **traffic signs** of a paragraph: they tell the reader what comes next — another idea? an opposite? a result? Learn them in **families**, by their job. One connector per sentence is enough.

**ES:** Los conectores son las **señales de tráfico** del párrafo: le dicen al lector qué viene — ¿otra idea? ¿lo contrario? ¿un resultado? Apréndelos en **familias**, según su trabajo. Un conector por oración es suficiente.

| Job / Trabajo | Connectors | Español |
|---|---|---|
| **Adding** / añadir | and, also, In addition, | y, también, además |
| **Contrast** / contraste | but, However, | pero, sin embargo |
| **Result** / resultado | so, As a result, | así que, como resultado |
| **Time** / tiempo | First, Then, After that, Finally, | primero, luego, después, finalmente |
| **Example** / ejemplo | For example, | por ejemplo |
| **Opinion** / opinión | I think (that), In my opinion, | creo que, en mi opinión |

**EN:** *And, but, so* join two engines **inside one sentence** (Unit 17). *However, In addition, As a result, For example* start a **new sentence**: after a full stop, with a **comma** after them.

**ES:** *And, but, so* unen dos motores **dentro de una oración** (Unidad 17). *However, In addition, As a result, For example* empiezan una **oración nueva**: después de un punto, con una **coma** después.

* ✓ I like my city, **but** it is expensive.
* ✓ I like my city**. However,** it is expensive.
* ✗ ~~I like my city, however it is expensive.~~ (a comma splice — Unit 17)

### A3 · Listing signals / Señales de enumeración

**EN:** When you give three reasons or three qualities, you have a list in your head. The reader cannot see it. So you put a small signal at the front of each item: *First, Second, Finally.* ★ The signal is **not in a seat**. It stands **outside the seats**, in front of the subject, with a **comma**. The seats behind it stay complete.

**ES:** Cuando das tres razones o tres cualidades, tienes una lista en la cabeza. El lector no la ve. Por eso pones una pequeña señal al principio de cada punto: *First, Second, Finally.* ★ La señal **no ocupa un asiento**. Se para **fuera de los asientos**, delante del sujeto, con una **coma**. Los asientos detrás quedan completos.

| (signal) | SUBJECT | VERB | OBJECT / DESCRIPTION |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | The hotel | is | clean. |
| **First,** | the hotel | is | clean. |
| **Second,** | the rooms | have | a view of the lake. |
| **In addition,** | breakfast | is | free. |
| **Finally,** | it | is | near the train. |

★ **The finger test / La prueba del dedo:** cover the signal with your finger. What stays must be a full sentence, with a subject and a verb. / Tapa la señal con el dedo. Lo que queda debe ser una oración completa, con sujeto y verbo.

* ✗ ~~Second, having a view of the lake.~~ (no subject, no working verb: a fragment)
* ✓ Second, **the rooms have** a view of the lake.

| Position / Posición | Best choice | Also possible | Español |
|---|---|---|---|
| item 1 | **First,** | First of all, · To begin with, | Primero, / En primer lugar, |
| item 2 | **Second,** | Next, · Also, | Segundo, / Luego, |
| one more | **In addition,** | Also, · Another reason is … | Además, / También, |
| last item | **Finally,** | Last of all, | Finalmente, / Por último, |

**Notes / Notas**

* *Of all* goes only with *first* and *last*: *first of all, last of all*. ✗ ~~second of all~~ (in writing). / *Of all* solo va con *first* y *last*.
* **Another** + one thing: *another reason*. **Other** + more things: *other reasons*. ✗ ~~other reason~~. / *Another* + singular; *other* + plural.
* **Also** moves. At the front, with a comma: *Also, it is cheap.* Inside the sentence, before the main verb and after *be*, with no commas: *It is **also** cheap. Buses **also** run at night.* / *Also* se mueve: al frente con coma; adentro, antes del verbo principal y después de *be*, sin comas.
* One signal per **item**, not per sentence. / Una señal por **punto**, no por oración.

**⚠ Spanish traps / Trampas del español**

| Español | ✗ Literal | ✓ English |
|---|---|---|
| En primer lugar, | In first place, | **First,** / First of all, |
| Al final (de la lista), | At the end, | **Finally,** |
| Otra razón es … | Other reason is … | **Another** reason is … |
| Por último, | For last, | **Finally,** / Last of all, |
| Además de eso, | Besides of that, | **In addition,** |

**EN:** *At the end* means *al final* of a place or a time: *at the end of the street*, *at the end of the movie*. It does not mark the last item of a list.

**ES:** *At the end* significa *al final* de un lugar o un tiempo: *at the end of the street* (al final de la calle), *at the end of the movie* (al final de la película). No marca el último punto de una lista.

**Listing order or time order? / ¿Orden de lista u orden de tiempo?** The same words do two jobs. In a **story**, *First, Then, Finally* follow the clock: *First, I woke up. Then, I ate breakfast.* In a **list of reasons**, they only give the number: *First, it is safe. Second, it is cheap.* In a list, put your **strongest** reason last — *Finally* is the one the reader remembers. / Las mismas palabras hacen dos trabajos. En una **historia**, siguen el reloj. En una **lista de razones**, solo dan el número. En una lista, pon tu razón **más fuerte** al final.

### A4 · Two paragraph skeletons / Dos esqueletos de párrafo

**EN:** Build your paragraph from a skeleton, one sentence at a time. Here are two: a **story** (past tense, time order) and an **opinion** (present tense, listing order).

**ES:** Construye tu párrafo desde un esqueleto, una oración a la vez. Aquí hay dos: una **historia** (pasado, orden de tiempo) y una **opinión** (presente, orden de lista).

**Story paragraph / Párrafo de historia**

```
Last (summer / year / week), I + past verb + ...      <- topic sentence
First, I + past verb + ...
Then, I + past verb + ...
After that, I + past verb + ...
Finally, I + past verb + ...
It was + ADJECTIVE, and I + felt + ...                <- concluding sentence
```

*Last summer, my brother visited us in Chicago. First, we walked by the lake. Then, we went to the Field Museum. … It was a wonderful week, and I felt very happy.*

**Opinion paragraph / Párrafo de opinión**

```
In my opinion, + SUBJECT + is / should + ...          <- topic sentence
First, + SENTENCE.
Second, + SENTENCE.
For example, + SENTENCE.
Finally, + SENTENCE (the strongest reason).
For these reasons, I think (that) + SENTENCE.         <- concluding sentence
```

*In my opinion, adults should learn a second language. First, it is good for your brain. … For these reasons, I think it is never too late.*

### A5 · Fact or opinion? / ¿Hecho u opinión?

**EN:** A **fact** can be **checked**: with numbers, dates, a map, a schedule. *Chicago is in Illinois. The class starts at 6 p.m.* An **opinion** is what a person thinks; other people can disagree. *Chicago is the most beautiful city in the world.* Opinion signal words: *best, worst, better, beautiful, boring, should, I think*. The test question: **"Can I check this?"** Yes → fact. No, it depends on the person → opinion.

**ES:** Un **hecho** se puede **verificar**: con números, fechas, un mapa, un horario. *Chicago is in Illinois. The class starts at 6 p.m.* Una **opinión** es lo que una persona piensa; otras personas pueden no estar de acuerdo. *Chicago is the most beautiful city in the world.* Palabras que señalan opinión: *best, worst, better, beautiful, boring, should, I think*. La pregunta de prueba: **"¿Puedo verificarlo?"** Sí → hecho. No, depende de la persona → opinión.

**Mini-practice 3 / Mini-práctica 3** — Fact (F) or opinion (O)?

2. Arepas are better than pizza.
3. Lucía's class is on Tuesday and Thursday.
4. Everyone should visit Chicago in the summer.

### A6 · Email frames: formal and informal / Marcos de email: formal e informal

**EN:** An email is a short paragraph with a **frame** around it. The frame depends on the reader: a manager, a school or a company → **formal**; a friend or family → **informal**. ★ Choose one column and stay in it. Mixing the two is the classic mistake (✗ *Dear Ms. Rivera, … Love, Lucía*).

**ES:** Un email es un párrafo corto con un **marco** alrededor. El marco depende del lector: un jefe, una escuela o una empresa → **formal**; un amigo o la familia → **informal**. ★ Elige una columna y quédate en ella. Mezclar las dos es el error clásico (✗ *Dear Ms. Rivera, … Love, Lucía*).

| Part / Parte | Formal | Informal |
|---|---|---|
| Subject line / Asunto | Schedule change | Pictures from the lake! |
| Greeting / Saludo | Dear Ms. Rivera, | Hi Ana, |
| Opening / Apertura | I am writing to + verb … | Thanks for your message! |
| Body / Cuerpo | 2–4 short sentences | 2–4 short sentences |
| Question / Pregunta | Could you …? | Can you …? |
| Before the end / Antes del final | Thank you for your time. | Write soon! |
| Sign-off / Despedida | Best regards, + full name | Love, / See you, + first name |

**EN:** The **subject line** is short: 2–5 words, no full stop. A good subject line tells the reader what the email is about before they open it.

**ES:** El **asunto** es corto: 2–5 palabras, sin punto final. Un buen asunto le dice al lector de qué trata el email antes de abrirlo.

**Formal email skeleton / Esqueleto de email formal**

```
Dear Mr. / Ms. + LAST NAME,
I am writing to + base verb + ...
SENTENCE 1 (a detail).
Could you / Could I + base verb + ...?
Thank you for + NOUN / verb-ing + ...
Please let me know if + SENTENCE.
Best regards,
FIRST NAME + LAST NAME
```

⚠ After *Thank you for* and *I look forward to*, the verb wears **-ing** (Unit 15: after a preposition → -ing): *Thank you for **helping** me. I look forward to **hearing** from you.* ✗ ~~I look forward to hear from you.~~

⚠ Después de *Thank you for* y *I look forward to*, el verbo lleva **-ing** (Unidad 15: después de preposición → -ing): *Thank you for **helping** me* (Gracias por ayudarme). *I look forward to **hearing** from you* (Espero su respuesta). ✗ ~~I look forward to hear from you.~~

### A7 · Common mistakes / Errores comunes

| ✗ | ✓ | Why / Por qué |
|---|---|---|
| In first place, the park is big. | **First,** the park is big. | literal *en primer lugar* |
| At the end, it is cheap. | **Finally,** it is cheap. | *at the end* = place or time |
| Other reason is the price. | **Another** reason is the price. | *another* + singular |
| Second, having good teachers. | Second, **it has** good teachers. | the signal needs full seats |
| First the room is clean. | First**,** the room is clean. | comma after the signal |
| I like it, however it is far. | I like it**. However,** it is far. | *however* starts a new sentence |
| Also, it is also cheap. | **Also,** it is cheap. | one signal is enough |
| Dear Ana, … Love, Lucía *(to a manager)* | Dear Ms. Rivera, … Best regards, | don't mix the frames |
| I look forward to hear from you. | I look forward to **hearing** from you. | preposition *to* → -ing |
| My paragraph has no topic sentence. | Start with the general idea. | the reader needs the big idea first |

### Word tip / Consejo de palabras

**EN:** In an email, **Dear** is normal and polite — it is not romantic. *Dear Ms. Rivera* = *Estimada Sra. Rivera*. Use **Ms.** for a woman when you don't know if she is married (it is the safest choice at work), **Mr.** for a man. And after the title, use the **last name**: ✓ *Dear Ms. Rivera*, ✗ ~~Dear Ms. Laura~~.

**ES:** En un email, **Dear** es normal y cortés — no es romántico. *Dear Ms. Rivera* = *Estimada Sra. Rivera*. Usa **Ms.** para una mujer cuando no sabes si está casada (es la opción más segura en el trabajo), **Mr.** para un hombre. Y después del título, usa el **apellido**: ✓ *Dear Ms. Rivera*, ✗ ~~Dear Ms. Laura~~.

**Mini-practice 1:** c) — it covers a), b) and d).

**Mini-practice 2:** 1. full sentence ✓ · 2. **fragment** (no subject, no working verb → *Second, I learn new words every week.*) · 3. full sentence ✓

**Mini-practice 3:** 1. F · 2. O (*better*) · 3. F · 4. O (*should*)

**C1:** 1. True · 2. False (she only said "Sorry?" and "Thank you.") · 3. True · 4. False (it is not perfect) · 5. False (Mondays and Wednesdays) · 6. True

**C2:** 1. *My first year in Chicago changed my English in four important ways.* · 2. She understands people better. · 3. Five short sentences about herself. · 4. At 7 a.m., because her son has soccer practice at 4 p.m. (and she needs to take him). · 5. Formal. Two of: *Dear Ms. Rivera* · *I am writing to…* · *Could I…?* · *Thank you for your time* · *I look forward to hearing from you* · *Best regards* + full name.

**C3:** 1. *First, · Second, · In addition, · Finally,* · 2. example: *For example,* · contrast: *However,* · 3. *For these reasons, I think this was the most important year of my life.* — an opinion (*I think*, *the most important*) · 4. Three of: *Dear Ms. Rivera,* · *I am writing to ask for…* · *Could I work…?* · *Thank you for your time.* · *Please let me know if…* · *I look forward to hearing from you.* · *Best regards,*

**D (example, Task 1):** This year my English changed in three ways. First, I can talk to my neighbors. Last year, I only said "Hello." Now I talk to them about the weather and our kids. Second, I understand more on TV. For example, I watch the news in English every morning. I also understand some songs on the radio. Finally, and most important, I am not afraid of mistakes. I make many mistakes, but I learn from them. My teacher says this is normal. My English is not perfect. However, it is much better than last year. For these reasons, I think my year was a success.

**D (example, Task 2):** *Subject:* Class schedule · Dear Mr. Brown, I am writing to ask about the next English course. Could you tell me the days and times? I work until 5 p.m., so I need a night class. Thank you for your help. Please let me know if there is a class on Tuesdays. I look forward to hearing from you. Best regards, Pedro Gómez
