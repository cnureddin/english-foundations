# Roadmap — Parts 2 and 3 (to do)

**Goal of the whole series:** take an adult Spanish speaker from zero to **B2**, with a **C1** track at the end. Part 1 (A1 → A2) is written. This file is the to-do list and draft syllabus for what comes next. Same method (seats model, EN/ES, 3 × 45 min per week, graded story, answer keys), same template (`AUTHORING.md`), same characters moving forward in time.

Realistic time frames at 3 × 45 min per week (≈ 2.25 h/week of guided study, plus daily exposure): A2 → B1 ≈ 6 months, B1 → B2 ≈ 8–9 months, B2 → C1 ≈ 12+ months. At B2 and above, the book must push more independent reading and listening outside the book; the weekly plan should add one "free input" task (a podcast episode, an article, a TV episode) with a short written report.

## Part 3 · Independent to proficient (B1 → B2, with a C1 track) — ~30 weeks

**Shift in the method:** English-first; Spanish only for traps and hard concepts. Authentic texts (adapted) from week 1. Each week: 1 grammar/structure focus, 1 long reading (600–1,000 words), 1 writing task (250–350 words: essays, reports, reviews, formal letters), vocabulary from the reading (collocations, word families, academic words). A "free input" log becomes mandatory.

| Block | Grammar / structure | Skills focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Narrative tenses | past perfect, past perfect continuous; narrative tense mix | short stories, storytelling |
| 2 · The future in detail | future continuous, future perfect, *be about to / be due to* | predictions, plans at work |
| 3 · Conditionals and wishes | third & mixed conditionals; *wish / if only* (past); *I'd rather* | regret, hypotheses |
| 4 · Passive and causative | all tenses; *have/get something done*; *it is said that…* | reports, news |
| 5 · Reporting | reported speech (full), reporting verbs (*suggest, deny, admit*) | summaries, minutes |
| 6 · Complex sentences | non-defining relative clauses, participle clauses, noun clauses | academic reading |
| 7 · Modals 3 | past deduction (*must have, can't have*), *needn't have*, *should have* | speculation, advice |
| 8 · Linking and cohesion | contrast & concession (*although, despite, whereas*), reference words | essays: discursive, argument |
| 9 · Emphasis (C1 track) | inversion, cleft sentences (*What I need is…*), fronting | speeches, persuasive writing |
| 10 · Register (C1 track) | nominalization, hedging (*tend to, it appears that*), formal vocabulary | reports, academic writing |

Writing genres by the end of Part 3: opinion/discursive essay, report, review, formal letter/email, article, summary. Reading: long articles, opinion columns, short academic texts, one graded novel read across the part.
